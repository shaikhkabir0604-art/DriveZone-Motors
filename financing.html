How Car Financing Works

Step 1: Choose Your Car

Select a new or pre-owned car that suits your needs and budget.

Step 2: Discuss Your Budget

Decide how much you can pay as a down payment and what monthly instalment may be affordable.

Step 3: Explore Loan Options

Compare the interest rate, loan tenure, processing fees, and other applicable charges.

Step 4: Submit Your Application

Provide the documents requested by the relevant lender and complete the application process.

Step 5: Loan Approval

The lender reviews your application and determines whether to approve the loan.

Documents You May Need

  • Identity proof
  • Address proof
  • Income proof or salary slips, where applicable
  • Bank statements, if requested
  • Other documents required by the lender

Document requirements vary depending on the lender and applicant.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I explore financing for a pre-owned car?

Yes, you can ask our team about available financing options for pre-owned vehicles.

What is a down payment?

A down payment is the initial amount you pay towards the car's purchase price.

What is a loan tenure?

Loan tenure is the period over which you repay the loan.

Is loan approval guaranteed?

No. Approval depends on the lender's eligibility criteria and assessment.

How can I learn more about financing?

You can contact our team for guidance on the next steps.
